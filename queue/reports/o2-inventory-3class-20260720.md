## Summary

contain no resistance/eradication-specific logic in their `1c31ecc..HEAD` diffs (settings.yaml's

nothing calls it, and no dedicated resistance-pattern dashboard exists, so real work remains on the

supersede remediation (`753df7e`) correctly neutralized the two files/sections literally named
`FKI-NO-CHOICE-OFFER-01`/`FKI-MAX-STRENGTH-01`, but it missed three functionally-equivalent live
mechanisms that reproduce the same forbidden effect under different names —
`hakudokai_escalation.py`'s `auto_answer()`, `instructions/fukuincho.md`'s un-superseded
"選択肢自動回答 (L1_CHOICE)" section, and the hardcoded "抵抗パターン禁止" clause baked into
`hakudokai_init_agents.sh`/`hakudokai_secondpc_setup.sh` bootstrap prompts. These three are called
out explicitly under 禁止対象 above and should be escalated for the same supersede/removal
treatment already applied elsewhere, rather than treated as O-2 implementation targets.
